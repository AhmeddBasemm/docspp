## Check your work

Errors and warnings appear in the Problems panel as you type. They are the checks `docspp check` runs: unknown keys with a suggestion, edges to nodes that do not exist, steps with no route, icons that do not resolve and `refs:` that point at files that have moved.

**docspp: Check All Diagrams** compiles every diagram in the workspace at once. The status bar shows the state of the diagram you are editing; click it to see the problems.
