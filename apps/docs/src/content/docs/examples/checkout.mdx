A shop with a CDN, a gateway, three services, a cache, a database, a queue and a payment provider. It is the diagram behind most examples in these docs.

Things to try:

- Pick **Place an order** and press play, then switch to **Sequence** and **Story** to read the same steps two other ways.
- Click **API gateway** or **order-service** to open their notes.
- Open **Backend only** for the same model with the edge layer left out.
- Pick **Card declined**, and see how a failure is a scenario of its own.
- Hover a line, then the matching row of the interfaces table at the bottom.
